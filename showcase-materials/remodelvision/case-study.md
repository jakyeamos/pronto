# RemodelVision: make room constraints visible

This candidate case starts with a synthetic original room illustration and a
plain brief: keep the floor, stay near a $12k budget, and prioritize storage.
It shows three directions—warm minimal, color-forward, and storage-first—then
points at the preserved element and the tradeoff the brief is meant to shape.

The useful story is not “AI makes a renovation decision.” It is that a visual
workflow can make constraints inspectable while leaving the homeowner in
control. Any estimate-oriented handoff would still need assumptions, ranges,
date/location limits, and no-guarantee language.

This is a **candidate local package**. The illustration is synthetic and is not
a real property or current product output. RV-1 contribution approval and RV-3
runtime proof remain open, so no personal attribution, generated result, or
construction promise is being made.
