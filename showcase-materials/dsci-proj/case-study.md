# Dsci-proj: normalize a backlog before asking what comes next

This candidate case puts two backlog shapes beside one decision contract. A
research-issue backlog and a product backlog use different fields, but both can
map to a canonical item with evidence, state, and user-owned criteria.

The interesting product boundary is the contract, not an opaque score. The
fixture makes missing inputs fail closed, requires factor-level explanation, and
specifies a deterministic before/after scenario when a weight changes.

This is a **candidate local package**. The appendix is synthetic and explicitly
not current product output. DS-1 adapter implementation, live scenario proof,
current runnable evidence, hosting, and destination readbacks remain open.
