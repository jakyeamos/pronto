# Book: let motion follow the prose

The candidate chapter **The Signal Room** begins quietly, crosses a narrative
threshold, lets motion and sound gather around the sentence, and then returns
control to the reader. Four beats—quiet, threshold, transformation, and exit—
make the intended arc easy to inspect.

The reader contract matters as much as the atmosphere: mute, reduced motion,
keyboard navigation, and font scale should change the same scene gracefully.
The authoring story is a manifest that connects passage, timing, layers, and
controls without exposing admin access.

This is a **candidate local package** using a synthetic appendix. It is not the
product chapter or product authorship. BK-1 rights, AI/human contribution,
direct runtime proof, hosting, and destination readbacks remain open.
