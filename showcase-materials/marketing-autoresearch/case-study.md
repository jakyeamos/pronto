# Marketing Autoresearch case selection

## Headline case: a real portfolio brief that refuses to become a marketing claim

The case starts with a real maintenance question: which AI Showcase story
should be foregrounded next for evidence-seeking technical hiring teams, and
what proof gap most limits trust? The brief compares Quality Runner, AI
Workflow Leverage, and the AI Code Quality Stack. It does not pretend that a
project name or an attractive concept is market evidence.

The source registry is deliberately small and inspectable: a portfolio README,
the portfolio's current-project records, and public repository documentation.
Each future material claim must name its source identifier and retrieval date.
The brief also excludes BidCamp client work, Soundscape materials, private
paths, credentials, raw provider payloads, outreach, spend, and publication.

## Target demo

1. Open the real public-safe brief and show its question, candidate lanes, and
   success definition.
2. Show the allowed-source registry and the privacy review before any research
   adapter is enabled.
3. Run the report-only probe and show the honest result: missing observations,
   zero external mutations, and no fabricated recommendation.
4. Inspect the MA-2 blocker: the runtime accepts a profile set, not this brief,
   and its observations cannot yet carry source-to-claim IDs, retrieval dates,
   allowed-source checks, or uncertainty labels.
5. Show the synthetic appendix rejecting a private source, an unlinked claim,
   and a publish request. Keep it clearly labeled as a reproducibility appendix.
6. End at the human handoff. The report and publication refusal receipt remain
   future proof gates, not narration substitutes.

## Proof boundary

The brief and privacy review are local evidence. The no-live probe proves
report-only execution and zero external mutations, not research quality. The
synthetic fixture tests fail-closed contract behavior, not audience demand or
campaign performance. A local candidate page and binary preview make the
boundary inspectable, but they do not prove a hosted page, a report result, or
that any destination was posted.

## Reproducibility appendix

Keep `synthetic-fixture.json` as a short appendix. Its three negative cases
make the refusal boundary replayable offline while the real public portfolio
brief remains the only headline case.
