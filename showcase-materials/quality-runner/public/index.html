What this means for your team

The standards that guide your agents can audit what they produce.

Skills and agent rules already describe the output and execution your team values. Their implied failure modes can become concrete checks, giving you a measurable debt ledger and a plan to improve the repository against your own definition of quality.

  1. 01 Your standard Name what good looks like

    Skills, guides, and agent rules express the output quality and execution behavior your team values.

  2. 02 Prevented outcome Make the failure explicit

    Reviewed ingest identifies concrete negatives such as generic copy, missing states, or unsafe boundaries.

  3. 03 Checkable contract Choose how to inspect it

    Low-noise standards become deterministic rules; judgment-heavy standards become bounded review rubrics.

  4. 04 Concrete findings Measure the gap in the repo

    Results retain their skill, rule, file, line, evidence, severity, scope, and coverage.

  5. 05 Remediation plan Turn debt into bounded work

    Findings are grouped by standard, risk, and surface, then verified against the value that created them.

Reviewed compilation, not arbitrary execution. An ingest agent can propose a conservative pack from a Skill, guide, or preference document; a human reviews it; Quality Runner validates the non-executable pack and records its exact version and content hash. It does not run arbitrary Skill code, call a model, or silently invent rules during a scan.

What drives a finding

Values become inspectable evidence.

The interesting part is not the warning count. It is the chain from repository signal to selected standard, scoped rule, finding, and coverage receipt.

  1. 01 Read repository signals

    Infer relevance from the codebase and its declared work.

  2. 02 Select relevant packs

    Activate only the standards that fit the repository.

  3. 03 Evaluate scoped rules

    Inspect only the files and patterns owned by each pack.

  4. 04 Keep provenance attached

    Record pack, rule, evidence, scope, and coverage.

  5. 05 Review before changing code

    Resolve each row through a fix, disposition, or open work.
