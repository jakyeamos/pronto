Quality Runner’s job isn’t to make a warning count disappear. It helps decide what should actually change.

In this Tenure case, a branch comparison produced 684 candidates. Those were a review surface, not 684 confirmed defects.

The tempting route was a bulk cleanup. We rehearsed that route on a disposable snapshot: 127 files changed, 15 integration or type errors, and stronger behavior disappeared. So the shortcut was rejected before merge.

Instead, the work moved through semantic triage and five bounded repair lanes, each tied to regression evidence.

The final candidate at 83b1318e has seven gates recorded in the canonical historical report. A later provider readback recorded the dev branch at e3b81e1a containing that candidate.

Those proof levels stay separate. The case-study surface rendered locally at desktop and mobile widths, but that is not browser proof of the Tenure product. Deployment is still not verified.

The result isn’t a cleaner score. It’s a safer engineering decision with an auditable reason.
