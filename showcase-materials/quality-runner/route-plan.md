## 1. Ideal target

**North star:** show owners defining what quality means for a repository, trace
those values into scoped and explainable findings, then show a real repository
reconciliation moving from noisy evidence to bounded engineering decisions, a
rejected unsafe route, and exact-commit proof.

**Non-negotiable:** the eight-pack Quality Skills configuration is actual Tenure
baseline evidence. Scanner candidates cannot be presented as confirmed defects
or a simplistic burn-to-zero score. The story must distinguish raw output,
open actionable findings, source fixes, source-evidenced dispositions, local
verification, historical provenance, browser review, and deployment.

**Preview concept.** Repository values flow through an active standards pack
into a namespaced finding and coverage receipt; the real case beneath it rejects
an unsafe shortcut and ends at an exact-commit receipt. Headline: “Make quality
mean what your codebase values.”

**Narrative spine.** Customer value → prevented outcome → checkable contract →
concrete finding → debt plan → repository signals → selected standards →
explainable findings and coverage → 4,022-row baseline → bounded slices and
scheduled refreshes → source fixes or exact dispositions → 537 raw / 0 open →
supporting unsafe-route vignette → historical evidence boundary.
