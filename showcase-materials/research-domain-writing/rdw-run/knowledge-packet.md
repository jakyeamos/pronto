# Knowledge Packet — rdw-tatum-claim-boundary

## Task

- output_type: `stat_interpretation`
- audience: AI product and sports analytics evaluators
- domain: basketball

## Non-negotiables

- Allowed: Jayson Tatum played 74 games in the 2023–24 NBA regular season and
  averaged 26.9 points, 8.1 rebounds, and 4.9 assists per game
  (`fact-tatum-line`).
- Allowed: the reviewed records identify Tatum with Boston in a forward role
  (`fact-tatum-team-role`).
- Forbidden: the box-score line proves efficiency, defensive impact, elite
  status, total value, or a future projection.
- Required uncertainty: distinguish recorded box-score volume from the impact
  evidence that is not loaded.

## Metrics

- Games played records the cited sample size; it does not explain availability.
- Points per game records scoring volume; it omits efficiency, shot difficulty,
  usage, lineup context, and total impact.
- Rebounds per game records rebounding volume; it omits rebound chances,
  box-outs, and scheme.
- Assists per game records assists; it omits potential assists, turnovers,
  creation difficulty, and lineup effects.

## Context and limitations

The sources place Tatum with Boston in a forward role. Usage, possession,
lineup, matchup, tracking, on-off, and all-in-one impact evidence are unknown or
not loaded. The safe interpretation is therefore narrower than a complete
player evaluation.

## Template

State the metric, value, and sample; say what the metrics capture; identify
what they miss; preserve the limited role context; and end with the evidence
boundary.

## Style

Direct and analytical. Avoid hype. Preserve every number and uncertainty
marker; style edits may not introduce facts.
