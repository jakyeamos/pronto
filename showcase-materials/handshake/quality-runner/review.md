## What the viewer should see

The same standards that guide a repository become reviewed, scoped findings.
The real Tenure case starts at 4,022 raw findings and ends at 537 retained raw
rows with 0 open actionable findings. The page keeps raw output, dispositions,
provenance, and the historical evidence boundary visible.

## Role split and limits

The team defines quality. Quality Runner preserves and explains the evidence.
The human owns dispositions, repairs, and release. The numbers are historical;
they do not prove that the current checkout passes or that the page is deployed.
The synthetic fixture is only a reproducibility appendix.
