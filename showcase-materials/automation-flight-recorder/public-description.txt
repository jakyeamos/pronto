Automation Flight Recorder turns one declared development workflow into a bounded parent/child trace. The current dev checkpoint keeps causality, redaction, hashes, omissions, partial failure, cancellation, and rerun guidance explicit: a failed child stays failed, later work is marked not-run, and rerun eligibility is reviewable rather than implicit permission. The local envelope is inspectable without a telemetry backend; Gateboard integration and recovery breadth remain open.
