## Optional rehearsal gate

Projects do not need to pass this gate to publish a complete preview,
description, public no-auth page, and linked proof. Use it only when a video is
worth producing.

A project reaches `rehearsal_passed` only when:

1. its accepted gap ledger is closed with the required project-specific proof;
2. sanitized inputs, outputs, receipts, limitations, and current-surface proof
   satisfy the ideal target's proof gate;
3. every spoken and visible claim maps to a reviewed claim ledger;
4. a real or clearly labeled synthetic case completes in 45–90 seconds;
5. the shot list preserves creator, AI-runtime, and human-review ownership;
6. redaction has been checked against the actual capture surfaces;
7. recovery paths preserve the safety and evidence boundaries; and
8. timing is measured from a real dry run, with synthesized timing labeled when
   it precedes the final human delivery.

The required stage order is `ideal_target → concept_materials → build_gap →
gap_closure → evidence → final_materials → reviewed`. The optional
`video_enhancement` branch may begin after evidence capture.
