# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary user is a recruiter evaluating Jakye Amos's AI product work. They
need to understand what each project does, what was difficult, what evidence
supports the result, and which claims remain bounded without reconstructing a
repository or trusting an unexplained score.

## Product Purpose

Pronto is an evidence-first portfolio command center. Its AI Showcase turns
reviewed local project state into legible, attributable demonstrations while
keeping product readiness, material readiness, public eligibility, and proof
strength distinct. Success means a recruiter can identify the product value
quickly and inspect the evidence behind it without private access.

## Positioning

Pronto does not treat a polished artifact or high quality score as publication
authority. It joins portfolio presentation to explicit ownership, provenance,
gap-closure, and evidence contracts, so uncertainty and unverified claims stay
visible instead of being smoothed over for presentation.

## Operating Context

The Showcase is prepared from a local macOS portfolio workspace spanning many
repositories. Each eligible project follows one durable route from ideal target
through gap closure, evidence, rehearsal, production, and review. The resulting
case study, preview, description, and recording must make the same factual
promise.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pronto reads a reviewed fleet-level Showcase contract and projects it into
  the desktop interface and machine-readable summary.
- Public, private-client, blocked, supporting, and not-applicable work retain
  separate dispositions; the interface cannot infer publication authority.
- Product readiness, demo-material readiness, career signal, work disposition,
  next-step category, missing materials, and blockers remain distinct.
- Concept work may lead implementation, but it cannot raise evidence-backed
  readiness or be published as current behavior.
- Local source checks, branch identity, browser proof, and deployment proof are
  separate evidence levels.

## Evidence on Hand

The reviewed Showcase contract lives in `.pronto/showcase-goal.json`. When a
new repository enters the registered fleet and that valid contract exists,
Pronto adds a pending `unknown` Showcase goal row for review without inventing
readiness or publication authority. Durable targets and project packages live under
`showcase-materials/`. Quality Runner's
Tenure case includes a readable decision trail, machine-readable case packet,
semantic change ledger, and provenance receipt. Crop-safe previews, recordings,
and public hosted case-study pages remain incomplete and must not be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Lead with the strongest useful product story, then close the exact gaps it
  creates.
- Make the consequence understandable before presenting implementation detail.
- Let evidence strength determine wording, never the desired polish level.
- Preserve human authority over publication, attribution, and private work.
- Build one complete reference-quality project vertically, then promote its
  reusable patterns across the portfolio.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Recruiter-facing surfaces must support keyboard navigation, visible focus,
reduced motion, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and plain-language
explanations that do not require repository or Quality Runner expertise.
